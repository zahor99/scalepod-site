Questions

Before you ask.

What do I need?
A Claude plan that allows custom connectors. We send the connector link and an invite; setup takes minutes.
Does AI touch the logo?
No. The approved file is placed by geometry. AI is used only for scenes, garment colours and sharpening, and those are labelled.
Who sends the link?
You do. Your customer never hears from us; you get the e-mail when they answer.
