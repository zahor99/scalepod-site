Step by step

What happens in the conversation

01

The brief becomes a kit

Paste the customer's e-mail or brief and attach the logo, or point to their site. Claude sets up the items, sizes and decoration; ScalePOD keeps the exact logo file, shows it back and waits for your yes.

02

Placed on your blanks, in millimetres

Your own product photos or catalogue blanks. Each placement has a size and a position in millimetres, embroidery or print look, any garment colour, front or back.

03

One review link for your customer

Check it internally first, then forward one link. Your customer marks every item Accept, Change or Reject on a phone, with a note in plain words. No logins, no attachments, and you get an e-mail the moment they send their decisions.

04

Changes collected, not chased

"The small copper mark, not the full logo." The note arrives with the decision, only that item is redone, and the next round goes out on the same link.

05

Approved means ready to order

Every accepted item has its files: a layered Photoshop file, the logo at print size in 300 dpi and a placement sheet in millimetres, ready for your embroiderer or printer.

Questions

Before you ask

What do I need?
A Claude plan that allows custom connectors. We send you the connector link and an invite; setup takes a few minutes.
Can I use my own blanks?
Yes. Upload product photos or import them from your shop; ScalePOD measures each blank once so every placement lands at its real size.
Does AI touch the logo?
No. The approved file is placed by geometry. AI is used only for scenes, garment colours and sharpening the blank, and those are labelled.
How do I know my customer has answered?
You get an e-mail the moment they send their decisions, with what they accepted, what they want changed and their notes. The link goes from you, not from us.
